What you'll find

Every booth is run by a local Rogue Valley maker. Come for the shopping, stay for lunch and music.

    All ages are welcome, and dogs are always welcome too.

  • 🧵

    Classes from our artisans

    Hands-on classes taught by our makers, starting soon on the patio and in the bar.

  • 🎉

    Host a gathering

      About Tiffany

      For more than ten years, Tiffany Jackson dreamed of a place where people could always find Southern Oregon's handmade talent under one roof. In August 2026, that dream opened on Front Street in downtown Central Point.

      Tiffany is a maker herself, an up-cycler and crafter. She and her husband, Travis, have run their own business for many years, and they have a big family: six-plus adult children and three grandbabies. They know how much it means when a community supports its small businesses.

      Her hope is simple: that the connection and community last all year long, through every season. Next up is a makerspace where people can learn, create and connect.

      “My success is your success, and we're all successful together.”

      Tiffany Jackson, owner

        Make a day of it

        We're in the heart of downtown Central Point, close to Front Street favorites like Rogue Creamery, West Coast Hog Dogs and Buttercloud Bakery's coffeehouse.

        Questions

        Is it good for kids?

        Yes! Tiffany's Marketplace is family-friendly and welcomes all ages.

        How do I pay?

        Pay for everything at our register. We take cards and cash, and gift cards are available.

        Are dogs welcome?

        Always! Dogs are welcome here, maybe even more than some people.

        Where do I park?

        Park in front of the building or just north of it. For events, there's overflow parking north of Rogue Creamery and street parking along Manzanita, Laurel, N. 1st and N. 2nd streets.

        Is the market wheelchair accessible?

        Yes. The entrance is step-free and our restrooms are accessible.

        What happens when it's cold or rainy?

        We're adding more heaters, fire pits and clear curtains around the patio soon to keep our outdoor area warm and dry.

        Can I rent space for a party?

        Yes. Our patio and bar area can be rented for small parties and gatherings. Call us at (541) 821‑8882.

        Do you have a makerspace?

        It's being built now: a space for painting, crafting, sewing and making, with classes, craft parties, and day use or memberships.
