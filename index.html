      About Tiffany

      For more than ten years, Tiffany Jackson dreamed of a place where people could always find Southern Oregon's handmade talent under one roof. In August 2026, that dream opened on Front Street in downtown Central Point.

      Tiffany is a maker herself, an up-cycler and crafter, and she and her husband Travis have run their own business for many years. They know how much it means when a community supports its small businesses.

      Her hope is simple: that the connection and community last all year long, through every season. Look for holiday shopping, winter events and bonfire nights ahead.

      “My success is your success, and we're all successful together.”

      Tiffany Jackson, owner
